Business Studies tutoring FAQs

We want you to know exactly how tutoring could help before you decide.

Ask about Business Studies tutors
Do you cover GCSE Business and A-Level Business case studies?
Yes. Share the awarding body and current module so lessons can focus on appropriate case studies and written question types.
Can I request a Business Studies tutor for my exam board?
Yes. Tell us the exact qualification and awarding body such as AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, Eduqas or Cambridge International. We will check which suitable tutors can support that specification.
Are lessons one-to-one or in a physical centre?
Northamptonshire Tutoring provides online one-to-one tuition. Families can join from home in any Northamptonshire town or village.
What does online tutoring cost?
GCSE tuition starts from £23 per hour and A-Level tuition starts from £28 per hour. Subject specialists or higher-experience tutors may charge more. Fees and availability are confirmed before booking.
