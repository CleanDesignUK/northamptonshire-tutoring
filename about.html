About Northamptonshire Tutoring

A local-facing online tutoring service backed by North Wales Tutoring Group. We help families across Northamptonshire find one-to-one teaching support that fits the learner.

Enquire about lessons WhatsApp our team
Our vision

Delivering a better future, one lesson at a time.

Northamptonshire Tutoring is part of North Wales Tutoring Group, a UK online tutoring network. Our purpose is to make personalised academic support easier to access for students, parents and learners across Northamptonshire.

We focus on what helps a student move forward: understanding difficult concepts, asking questions without pressure, practising unfamiliar exam formats and building a routine that feels manageable.

We provide online tuition, rather than claiming to operate a physical tutoring centre or local school partnership.

What matters to us

Our approach to online tutoring

01 / Confidence

Questions are welcome

Students should feel comfortable asking why an answer works, not just remembering steps.

02 / Structure

Relevant learning goals

We encourage lessons built around the learner’s year group, course specification and priority topics.

03 / Access

Learning from anywhere

Online delivery helps connect students in towns and villages to a broader tutor network.

Who we support

Maths, Physics and more, from primary school to sixth form

Our tutor network supports KS1–KS3, Year 6 SATs, GCSE and A-Level pupils. Popular requests include GCSE Maths, GCSE Combined Science, A-Level Maths, A-Level Physics, Chemistry and English.

We take account of AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, Eduqas and Cambridge International syllabuses where relevant. If your subject isn't listed, you can still ask us about options.
