Keeping children and young people safe

Northamptonshire Tutoring, part of North Wales Tutoring Group, takes safeguarding seriously throughout online lessons, tutor matching, communications and the management of concerns.

Safeguarding policy

Our safeguarding commitments and procedures

Every concern deserves to be taken seriously. The following explains our approach for families, tutors and learners across Northamptonshire.

Raise a concern
01

Our commitment

We work to promote the welfare of learners and respond appropriately to information suggesting a child may be at risk. Protecting learners takes priority over any commercial considerations. This policy covers tuition, tutoring-related messages, learning platforms and associated contact.

02

People, roles and responsibilities

Jesse is the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) for our group. Tutors and other people providing tuition must maintain professional boundaries, report concerns promptly, preserve factual records and co-operate with safeguarding processes. Our tutoring provider remains responsible for safeguarding processes it controls.

03

Tutor suitability, DBS and vetting

We carry out role-appropriate suitability checks, which may include identity, qualifications or experience, references, safeguarding declarations and DBS information where legally appropriate. A DBS certificate is not a guarantee of future conduct; appropriate professional boundaries and ongoing oversight also matter.

04

Online tutoring safeguards

Tutors must communicate professionally, use agreed channels and avoid inappropriate private contact with learners. Sessions, messages, file sharing and learning tools should be age-appropriate. Parents and carers should have a clear route to report uncomfortable or inappropriate behaviour.

05

Recognising and reporting concerns

Concerns may include neglect, abuse, exploitation, discrimination, bullying, inappropriate messages, grooming, boundary breaches or other behaviours that put a learner at risk. You do not need proof before telling us or contacting authorities.

06

Listening to a learner

Listen calmly, do not promise secrecy, avoid leading questions, record the learner’s own words where possible and report promptly. Do not investigate allegations yourself or circulate sensitive details unnecessarily.

07

Concerns about a tutor or the DSL

You can report any tutor-related concern using the confidential form. If the concern relates to Jesse or you prefer not to report internally, contact Northamptonshire Children’s Trust, the police or the relevant safeguarding authority directly. You do not need our permission to make an external referral.

08

Recording and information sharing

We keep factual, proportionate safeguarding records and share information only where appropriate for protecting learners or meeting legal obligations. Safeguarding information is handled with restricted access and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

09

Responding and escalating

We may restrict tutoring arrangements while a concern is assessed, seek specialist advice, notify relevant authorities or report allegations where required. We do not replace statutory investigations, decide whether crimes occurred or promise to keep a concern confidential when safety requires sharing.

10

Policy review and statutory context

We review safeguarding procedures at least annually and when circumstances or relevant guidance change. Our procedures take account of the safeguarding framework applicable in England, including Children Act provisions, Working Together to Safeguard Children, Keeping Children Safe in Education 2026 where relevant, and local safeguarding partnership procedures.
