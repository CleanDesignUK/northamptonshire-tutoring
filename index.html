Frequently asked questions

Online tutoring in Northamptonshire: subjects, pricing and how to start

We know choosing a tutor matters. Find the key answers before you contact us.

Ask us about a tutor
What subjects can I book an online tutor for in Northamptonshire?
Our wider NWT Group network covers Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Combined Science, English Language, English Literature, Economics, Business, Computer Science, History, Geography, Religious Studies, Psychology, Sociology, French, Spanish, Chinese, Welsh, Art & Design, Health & Social Care and more. Tell us your subject and level so we can check the most suitable tutor.
How much does GCSE or A-Level tutoring cost?
One-to-one GCSE tutoring starts from £23 per hour and A-Level tutoring starts from £28 per hour. Some specialist subjects and tutors cost more. We always confirm the tutor, lesson fee and availability before you book.
Do you offer GCSE Maths and Physics tutoring in Northamptonshire?
Yes. We support GCSE Maths, Further Maths and Physics for students in Northampton, Kettering, Corby, Wellingborough, Rushden, Daventry, Towcester, Brackley and surrounding towns, with lessons delivered online.
Can you match tutors to AQA, Edexcel and OCR exam boards?
Yes. Tell us the awarding body, specification, year group and any course options such as Foundation or Higher tier. Our network also supports WJEC, Eduqas and Cambridge International where suitable.
Can we get help with 11+, SATs, TMUA or UCAT preparation?
You can enquire about Year 6 SATs, 11+, 13+, TMUA, UCAT and selected entrance or admissions tests. We match the specific test requirements against tutor expertise and availability.
Do you offer lessons at home or in a Northamptonshire centre?
Northamptonshire Tutoring offers online lessons for families across the county. We do not advertise a local physical tutoring centre. Students study from home with a suitable online tutor.
Do I need to subscribe or commit to a long contract?
No subscription is needed. We discuss subject, tutor, lesson price and preferred times before you decide whether to book.
How do I start my first lesson?
Complete the short form with your name, email, phone and a message such as Year 10 GCSE Maths Higher Tier AQA. We will discuss suitable tutor options and how to arrange your first lesson.
