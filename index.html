FAQs

Questions about online tutoring in Northamptonshire?

Here are answers to common questions about subjects, prices, exam boards and booking.

Speak to our team
Do you offer online GCSE Maths and Physics tutoring in Northamptonshire?
Yes. We offer one-to-one online tutoring in GCSE Maths, GCSE Physics and other subjects for pupils in Northampton, Kettering, Corby, Wellingborough, Rushden, Daventry, Towcester and nearby areas.
How much does online tutoring cost?
Lessons up to GCSE level start from £23 per hour. A-Level lessons start from £28 per hour. We will confirm the rate for the suitable tutor and subject before booking.
Which exam boards do your tutors cover?
Our tutor network supports AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, Eduqas and Cambridge International (CAIE) specifications. Tell us your qualification and exam board when you enquire.
Are lessons online or in person?
Northamptonshire Tutoring currently offers online one-to-one lessons. We do not advertise an in-person Northamptonshire tutoring centre.
Can we request a tutor for GCSE Combined Science?
Yes. Please tell us whether you need Biology, Chemistry, Physics or a combination, plus your exam board and current year group.
Can I choose a regular weekly time?
You can request preferred days and times. We will check a suitable tutor’s availability before confirming a recurring slot.
How do we book our first lesson?
Use our enquiry form or WhatsApp us with the subject, year group, exam board and preferred availability. We will discuss a suitable tutor, rate and next steps.
