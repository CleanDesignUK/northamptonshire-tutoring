Who we are

Northamptonshire Tutoring is part of North Wales Tutoring Group. For questions about personal information submitted through this website, contact northamptonshire@nwttutoring.com.

Information we collect

When you complete our enquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, the student's year group, subjects, course information and the details you choose to include in your message. Please avoid providing sensitive medical or personal details through the enquiry form.

How we use that information

We use enquiry information to reply to you, discuss tuition options, determine whether suitable tutors may be available and arrange lessons if requested. We may retain appropriate administrative records for managing enquiries and lessons. We do not use contact-form details to send unrelated marketing messages without an appropriate basis.

Our legal bases

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to take steps you request before entering into tutoring arrangements, to fulfil agreed services or for legitimate interests in handling genuine enquiries. Other processing may be required to meet legal obligations.

Service providers and transfers

Our contact form uses Web3Forms, which processes submitted form data and forwards enquiries to our email. Information may be processed outside the UK, including by service providers. Where relevant, we consider the safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

Retention and your rights

We aim to keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated purposes and applicable record-keeping duties. You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of your data where the law provides these rights. Contact us using the email above. You may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Website storage

We use browser storage to remember your cookie preference. See our cookie policy for more information.

We may update this policy when our services or providers change. Please review this page for the latest version.
